Settings & Access

Role-based access

Your current demo role is Administrator.

  • Administrator: can add and edit patient records, manage appointments, and access all progress notes.
  • Staff: can view patient information and manage appointments and progress notes; user and system settings are restricted.

Data and privacy

Sample records are stored in this browser for demo purposes. This prototype does not provide real server-side authentication, encrypted clinical storage, backups, or audit-grade security.
